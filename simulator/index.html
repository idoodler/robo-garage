      - if:
          condition:
            switch.is_on: vent_auto
          then:
            - lambda: |-
                if (id(drawer_reed).state) {                       // drawer out: no fan while you work at the station
                  if (id(rear_fan).state) id(rear_fan).turn_off().perform();
                  return;
                }
                const float h = id(cab_hum).state, t = id(cab_temp).state;
                if (std::isnan(h) || std::isnan(t)) return;
                const float h_on = id(vent_hum_on).state, t_on = id(vent_temp_on).state;
                static bool running = false;
                if (h > h_on || t > t_on) running = true;
                else if (h < h_on - 5.0f && t < t_on - 1.0f) running = false;   // 5 %RH / 1 degC hysteresis
                if (!running) {
                  if (id(rear_fan).state) id(rear_fan).turn_off().perform();
                  return;
                }
                // 30 % at the switch-off point, 100 % at limit + 15 %RH or + 6 degC, in 10 % steps
                const float x = std::max((h - h_on + 5.0f) / 20.0f, (t - t_on + 1.0f) / 7.0f);
                const float s = 30.0f + 70.0f * std::min(std::max(x, 0.0f), 1.0f);
                const int speed = 10 * (int) roundf(s / 10.0f);
                if (!id(rear_fan).state || id(rear_fan).speed != speed)
                  id(rear_fan).turn_on().set_speed(speed).perform();
  - interval: 10min                                            # beam self-test while the door is idle
    then:
      - if:
          condition:
            lambda: 'return id(door).current_operation == cover::COVER_OPERATION_IDLE;'
          then:
            - script.execute: beam_selftest

# Optional VL53L1X (robot in bay) on the I2C clamp: needs an external ESPHome component.
        Build notes
        • Stand the cabinet 4 cm from the wall: exhaust for the rear fan and room for cables down the back-right corner.
        • No METOD bottom panel – the slides sit at the very bottom and the tray rides 3 mm above the floor. Robot path: floor → wedge (1.5 cm, ≈9°) → tray → the station's own ramp plate (+1.2 cm).
        • Floor-safe pull-out: the loaded drawer weighs ≈30 kg. At full extension the slides sag a few mm – more than the 3 mm gap, so tray and wedge would scrape. Fix: 2 soft PU wheels (Ø30 mm) in the front corners, just inside the cheeks. They touch the floor and roll; the slides carry the back and keep it straight. Nothing else touches the floor. Keep the floor in front clean (grit under a wheel can still mark soft parquet).
        • Everything on the drawer stays below 62 cm, so it slides out under the fixed fascia. Electronics, adapter, strip and fan live in the fixed space above the station.
        • Lift door: PTFE tape in the channels; if it ever tilts, add a second actuator on the right, wired in parallel. Every door: the light barrier keeps it from closing on anything in front; the INA219 stall stop on the PCB is the backup.
        • Parallel lift: 4 arms, 10 cm hole to hole (12 cm pieces), all pivots on M6 shoulder bolts.
        • Stable pull-out: a 100 N pull on the handle (57 cm high) levers ≈56 N·m onto the tray joint. With 10 cm cheeks that is ≈350 N on the rear screws of each cheek – MDF end grain loosens. Now: 18 mm birch plywood cheeks with a 25 cm foot (≈120 N per cheek), glued + 5 screws from below; side rails glued + screwed every 8 cm; top rail ties the cheek tops and takes 3 screws into VEDDINGE; 2 small steel angles per side hold VEDDINGE at the bottom.
        • Clean button: Ø19 mm hole in the fascia, right side; from behind a Ø30 mm recess 8 mm deep so the nut grips (thread is shorter than 18 mm). Wire it to the BUTTON clamp (BTN, GND, 12V, LED−): GPIO19 reads the switch, a MOSFET on the PCB switches the ring (GPIO23). The fascia is fixed, so no wires through the drag chain.
        • Light barrier: sender + receiver on 2 small L-brackets at the front corners of the cabinet sides. Beam ≈12 cm in front of the door, ≈2 cm above the floor – past the ramp wedge and under the door's swing, so the door never breaks it itself. Both cables go to the BEAM clamp.
        • Controller: PCB in its printed case under the top, the vented SHT40 corner open to the cabinet air. Keep those vents free.
        • IKEA fronts: VEDDINGE 60×40 (16 mm) sits on 2 mm shims, so it is flush with the 18 mm fascia, door and legs. Its top edge stays at 62.2 cm; the door row below is 1.5 cm taller to match. Top: JÄRSTORP 186 cm cut to 60 cm (63.5 deep → 1.6 cm overhang), cut edges sealed.
        • Still open: measure the docked depth (robot nose → back of station). It must be ≤ 55 cm.
